# Subject-Adaptive Azerbaijani-Safe Typography Matrix

Every chosen font **must** render full Azerbaijani Latin Extended characters cleanly (`Ə, ə, Ğ, ğ, İ, ı, Ö, ö, Ş, ş, Ü, ü, Ç, ç`) cleanly and look natural and effortless to the human eye.

---

## 1. Curated Eye-Friendly Discipline Pairings

Prefer clean, human-readable modern sans-serif and balanced editorial fonts. Avoid cramped or overly ornate display fonts for dense educational content.

| Discipline / Tone | Display / Heading Font | Body / Data Font | Character & Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Academic Science, Medicine & Anatomy** | `Segoe UI` (Semibold/Bold) | `Segoe UI` (or `Calibri`) | Clean, natural legibility for Azerbaijani diacritics, Latin anatomical terms, and long reading comfort. |
| **Engineering, IT & Architecture** | `Bahnschrift` / `Segoe UI` | `Segoe UI` (or `Aptos`) | Precision geometry paired with crisp technical readability. |
| **Business, Strategy & Economics** | `Calibri` / `Segoe UI` | `Calibri` / `Aptos` | Balanced executive clarity without visual fatigue. |
| **History, Literature, Humanities** | `Georgia` (or `Calibri`) | `Calibri` / `Segoe UI` | Warm classical authority paired with effortless body reading. |
| **Art, Culture, Design & Museums** | `Palatino Linotype` / `Segoe UI` | `Segoe UI` | Refined editorial proportions that frame imagery calmly. |
| **Botanical, Ecology & Geography** | `Calibri` / `Constantia` | `Calibri` / `Segoe UI` | Airy, organic legibility across text and labeled maps. |

---

## 2. Scale & Hierarchy Rules (16:9 Canvas: `960 x 540` pt)

- **Hero Cover Title**: `40 – 48 pt` (Bold, clean natural phrasing—no punctuation clutter)
- **Content Slide Title**: `26 – 30 pt` (Bold, single consistent top position across content slides)
- **Primary Body Copy**: `16 – 18 pt` (Line spacing `1.28 – 1.35`; **never drop below `15 pt`** on content slides)
- **References Slide Text**: `15 – 16 pt` (Clean readable bibliography—no tiny footnotes)
- **Minimum Font Size Floor**: **Zero `<14 pt` micro-text anywhere on slides.** Never place tiny eyebrow kickers (`01 • SECTION`), small metadata strips, or micro captions beneath figures.

---

## 3. Consistent Placement & Balance Rules

- **Consistent Grid**: Keep titles, primary body text columns, and visual frames locked to steady coordinates across content slides so the viewer's eye never jumps erratically from slide to slide.
- **Clean Punctuation**: Write natural words instead of `~`, `•` separator chains, `|`, or `—` clutter inside titles and headings.
- **Line Width**: Keep body columns around `400–450 pt` wide (`~50–65` characters per line) with generous whitespace and clear `>= 4.5:1` contrast on a unified background.
